About Toadstool

Toadstool is a game about a toad, a lab, and a lot of mushrooms. You do all of the lab work yourself: agar, culturing, setup, and running your own mycology lab to grow mushrooms.

Early access has every mushroom in a small simulator. You get the whole lab and an outside area, NPCs to talk to, a library to look around, and orders you can place to have supplies delivered to the lab.

A full storyline is coming in about six to nine months. Early supporters will get an early access badge later on.
