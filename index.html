Toad Stool Labs

Our game. You run a small mushroom lab that is quietly trying to ruin your work.

Pour agar, take spore prints, move cultures onto grain and keep the air clean — because one careless week of contamination takes the whole shelf with it.

Every species in the game is a real one, with its own body, growth habit and substrate. The lab, the tools, the shaders and the frog who works there are all made here.
