Toad Stool Labs

Grow real mushrooms in a lab that is quietly trying to ruin them. Pour agar, take spore prints, keep the air clean, and watch one bad week of contamination eat the whole shelf.

Every species is a real one, with its own body, growth habit and substrate. The lab, the tools, the shaders and the frog who works there are all made here.
