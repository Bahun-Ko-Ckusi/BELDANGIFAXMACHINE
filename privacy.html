Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Beldangi Fax Machine LC (“we”, “us”) collects through the video game Toadstool (also shown in the game as “Toadstool Labs”) and our websites: the Toadstool site and the Beldangi Fax Machine LC site. The short version: the game sends only what its online features need, most of it is deleted within a day, we run no ads or tracking, and we don’t sell anything about you.

1. What stays on your computer

Your saves, settings and frog are stored on your own computer. The game also makes a random player number the first time it runs and keeps it there. It is not linked to your name or your Steam account. The game’s content warning does not record anything about you.

2. What the game sends online

The game’s online features use a server we run on Supabase, a database hosting company. When the game is connected to the internet:

  • Character looks. At start the game downloads the looks of the game’s townsfolk from our server. Like any internet request, the server receives your IP address and basic technical information to answer it.
  • Player record. While you are in a world, the game saves a small record on our server with a player ID (your Steam ID when Steam is running, otherwise a random number), your frog’s name, your frog’s look and the time you were last seen. It is deleted automatically about 24 hours after you stop playing.
  • Friendships with townsfolk. How friendly the game’s characters are with you is saved on our server under your random player number, so it carries over between sessions. It is deleted automatically after 24 hours without a change.
  • Bug notes. If you leave a note with the in-game Bug Clipboard, we receive the text you write, your frog’s name and where in the game you were. We keep notes until we have dealt with them. Please don’t put personal information in a bug note.
  • Playing together. With Steam running, your frog’s name, look, position and actions are sent to the friends in your lobby through Steam’s network. Without Steam, the game shares the same things through our server with other players who are online at the same time. These live updates are not stored, apart from the player record above.

Player records, friendship records and bug notes are shared by the game’s online features, so other copies of the game can read them. Use a frog name you are happy for other players to see.

3. Steam

The game is sold and delivered through Steam and uses Steam features such as friends, lobbies and invites. Your Steam account, date of birth, purchases, payments and achievements are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy. We don’t receive your payment details or your date of birth. Steam gives developers combined sales and usage reports that don’t identify you personally.

4. Our websites

  • No account is needed, and we run no analytics, ads or tracking cookies.
  • Both sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type to run and secure the service.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads.

5. If you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and keep it only as long as we need to. Ask us and we’ll delete it.

6. How we use information

We use the information above only to run the game’s online features, fix bugs, keep online play safe and reply to you. We don’t use it for advertising or build profiles of players.

7. Who we share it with

We don’t sell or rent your personal information. It is handled for us by the services named above (Supabase for the game’s server, Valve for Steam, GitHub for website hosting, Google for fonts), which may process it in the United States and other countries. We share it with anyone else only if the law requires us to.

8. Adults only

Toadstool contains mature content and is for adults aged 18 and over. The game and our websites are not directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has sent us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

9. Your choices

You can play without any of the online features by playing with your computer offline. You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. Tell us your frog’s name or Steam ID so we can find it. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or US state privacy laws, and we’ll honour them.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no method of sending or storing data is completely secure.

11. Other sites

Our sites link to other services, including Steam, Instagram, TikTok and Linktree. Their privacy practices are their own.

12. Changes

We may update this policy, for example if we add online features. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it last changed at the top. If a change affects what the game sends, we’ll update this policy before that game update ships.
