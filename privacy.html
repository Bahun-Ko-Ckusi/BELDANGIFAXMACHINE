Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Beldangi Fax Machine LC (“we”, “us”) collects through the video game Toadstool (also shown in the game as “Toadstool Labs”) and our websites: the Toadstool site and the Beldangi Fax Machine LC site. The short version: we collect as little as we can, we don’t track you, and we don’t sell anything about you.

1. The game

Toadstool does not send us your personal information. Your saves and settings are stored on your own computer. The game’s content warning does not record anything about you.

If we add online features later, such as crash reports or anything else that sends data to us, we will update this policy before that update ships and explain what is collected.

2. Steam

The game is sold and delivered through Steam. Your Steam account, date of birth, purchases, payments, achievements and any Steam features are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy. We don’t receive your payment details or your date of birth. Steam gives developers combined sales and usage reports that don’t identify you personally.

3. Our websites

  • No account is needed, and we run no analytics, ads or tracking cookies.
  • Both sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type to run and secure the service.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads.

4. If you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and keep it only as long as we need to. Ask us and we’ll delete it.

5. Sharing

We don’t sell or rent your personal information. We share it only if the law requires us to.

6. Adults only

Toadstool contains mature content and is for adults aged 18 and over. Our websites are not directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has sent us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

7. Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or US state privacy laws, and we’ll honour them.

8. Other sites

Our sites link to other services, including Steam, Instagram, TikTok and Linktree. Their privacy practices are their own.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it last changed at the top.
