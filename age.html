18+ Content Notice

Last updated November 20, 2026

Toadstool is for adults aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, do not buy, download or play it. This page lists the mature content in the game so you can decide before you buy. It is part of our Terms of Service.

What the game contains

  • Drug content. Growing, harvesting, preparing and taking psilocybin (“magic”) mushrooms and other drugs, and the hallucinations that follow.
  • Flashing lights. Intense flashing colours, glitches and heavy visual distortion, especially during trips. This may affect people with photosensitive epilepsy.
  • Motion. Swaying, warping and moving visuals that can cause motion sickness.
  • Mature themes. Altered states of mind and psychological themes that some players may find upsetting.
  • Online play. Playing with Steam friends, where other players can see your frog’s name and look.

Short description

Toadstool is for adults 18+. It depicts the growing and use of psilocybin ("magic") mushrooms and other drugs, including drug-induced hallucinations shown with heavy visual distortion and intense flashing colours. Drug use is fictional and not presented as a guide. The game does not encourage illegal activity. Some scenes may affect players with photosensitive epilepsy.

It is fiction, not advice

Psilocybin is a Schedule I controlled substance under United States federal law and is illegal in many other countries. Nothing in the game is a real guide, recipe or set of instructions, or medical, legal or scientific advice. Mushrooms in the game are not accurate enough to identify real ones. Never pick, eat or give anyone a wild mushroom because of anything you saw in the game. If someone may have eaten a poisonous mushroom, get medical help immediately (in the US, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 or 911).

Parents and guardians

Toadstool is marked as mature content on Steam. Steam asks for a date of birth before showing mature store pages and lets you block mature games with Steam’s family and parental controls. We don’t verify anyone’s age ourselves, so please use those controls to keep the game away from children.
